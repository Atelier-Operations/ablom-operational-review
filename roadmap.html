Debora and partners

  1. Oct 26

    Product selection criteria agreed with Davis: audience fit, margin, partner fulfilment capacity, lead time, earlier sale

    Audience data requested from Debora: demographics, engagement, top content, past brand work

  2. Oct 30

    Longlist of 5–6 products in textured hair care, fragrance and beauty, including Brazilian and Korean brands; brand, price point and audience fit recorded for each

  3. Nov 6

    Shortlist narrowed to 1–2 products against the criteria above

    Media kit v1 and one-page partner pitch completed

    Decision-maker identified at each selected brand

  4. From Nov 9

    Outreach starts to the brands behind the selected products, beginning with Brazilian and Korean beauty brands

  5. Mid-November

    Debora signs (date not confirmed); media kit updated with data shared after signing

  6. Nov 16–30

    Outreach continues with the media kit and pitch

  7. Nov 25

    Partner term sheet ready: commission or revenue share, promo mechanics, content deliverables and usage rights, exclusivity, timing, payment schedule, invoicing

  8. Nov 27

    Black Friday: teaser or announcement only

  9. Dec 1–15

    Terms negotiated with brands that responded: commission or revenue share, promo mechanics, stock and shipping capacity, content rights, timing

  10. Dec 1–20

    First Debora activation in the holiday gifting window; fallback January 2027

  11. Dec 15

    First partner contract signed by the brand, Debora and ABLOM

ABLOM brand and presence

  1. Oct 23

    Positioning defined: who ABLOM serves, why creators join, difference from an agency or incubator

  2. Nov 6

    Vision rewrite, draft

    Brand kit v1: logo, colours, typography, tone of voice

  3. Nov 13

    LinkedIn company page live

    Pitch deck v1

  4. Nov 16–30

    Brand kit applied to the deck; first LinkedIn posts

  5. Nov 30

    Pitch deck final

    One-page website live: what ABLOM is, who it is for, contact

  6. Early December

    ABLOM soft launch (date and format to be confirmed)

  7. December

    First Debora story added to the website; LinkedIn posts continue

Contracts and business model

  1. Oct 30

    Benchmark of Night Ventures, Banger Ventures, Beast Industries and one or two beauty/CPG incubators: economics with creators, owned versus outsourced, time from signing to first product

  2. Nov 2–13

    Business model review: ABLOM share, margin, commissions, cash timing; 12-month target of €2M ecosystem volume re-checked

  3. Nov 13

    Debora agreement ready for signature; ABLOM share of relevant commercial revenue 15–20% (brief)

  4. Nov 16 – Dec 4

    Operating model draft; recommendation on Dec 4: owned, orchestrated and outsourced; partners needed; changes at €100K, €500K and €1M+ revenue

  5. Dec 8–23

    Business model re-check with actual partner terms: ABLOM share, margin, commissions, cash timing

  6. Dec 28

    Retrospective with Davis; Q1 2027 priorities set; recommendation for the next collaboration cycle

  7. Dec 31

    Three SOPs completed: product validation, creator onboarding, partner pitching

Founder project: lighting object

  1. Phase 1

    Brief and supplier list defined; price, margin and production lead time checked before any spend

  2. Dec 31

    Decision to validate, pause or postpone, based on supplier quotes, price and margin
